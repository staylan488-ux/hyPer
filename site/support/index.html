Common questions

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder, and that the address was typed correctly. The email comes from hyPer's sign-in service. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, try signing up again with the same address, or write to us.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. Open the link in the email on your iPhone and it takes you back to the app to choose a new one. Only the most recent link works.

Why is an AI estimate wrong?

AI estimates are guesses from a photo or description, and portions are the hardest part. A second photo from the side, or a few words such as "about 200 g of rice", helps. You can always change an estimate before you log it. See Terms of Use for when not to rely on one.

It says I've reached today's AI limit

Each account can make a set number of AI requests a day, and there is also a daily budget shared by everyone. The count starts over at midnight UTC (7 or 8 pm Eastern time). Search, barcode and manual entry keep working in the meantime.

How are my targets worked out?

From your sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal, using standard energy-expenditure formulas. If adaptive targets are on, hyPer adjusts them over time from what you log and your weight trend. You can change your targets yourself at any time under You › Targets. They are estimates, not medical advice.

Apple Health isn't bringing in my weight

In the app, go to You › Connections and turn Apple Health on. If you turned it off earlier, open the iOS Health app, go to your profile, then Apps › hyPer, and allow Weight. hyPer only reads weight; it never writes to Apple Health.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to You, tap your name at the top, then Delete account. Everything in the account is deleted permanently. If you can't get into the app, email us from your account's address and we'll do it for you. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can I use kilograms?

Body weight and food can be entered in metric units. Training loads are in pounds for now.

Is there an iPad or Android version?

Not yet. hyPer is made for iPhone.
