Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

hyPer is an iPhone app for logging workouts and food. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else receives it, and how to delete it.

hyPer is made by [SELLER NAME] ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your data, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL].

The short version

  • We collect what you put into the app (workouts, food, weight, targets) so it can be saved to your account and shown on your other devices.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. The app contains no advertising or analytics tools.
  • When you ask for an AI food estimate, the photo or description you send goes to Google's Gemini service to be analyzed. The app asks your permission before the first time this happens.
  • Your run routes stay on your phone. Only the distance, time and splits are saved to your account.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Your account

Your email address and password (stored only in scrambled, one-way form by our sign-in provider), and the display name you choose. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address they share with us. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the relay address Apple gives us.

What you log

  • Training: programs, workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, rest times, notes, and other activities you record.
  • Food: the foods and meals you log, their nutrition values, the time you ate them, saved foods and meals, and foods you create.
  • Body and goals: your weigh-ins, and the details used to work out calorie and protein targets: sex, age (stored as birth year), height, optional body-fat estimate, activity level, goal and rate of change, and the targets themselves.
  • Runs: when you record a run, its distance, duration, pace and laps or sprint repetitions. The GPS route itself is used on your phone to measure the run and is not sent to us.

Apple Health

If you allow it, hyPer reads your body-weight measurements from Apple Health and saves them to your account so your weight trend and targets stay current. hyPer does not write anything to Apple Health. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or sold, and is shared only as described in this policy to provide features you ask for.

Location and motion

The run tracker uses your precise location, including while the screen is locked, and your phone's motion sensors, only while a run you started is in progress. This is processed on your phone. We receive the totals described above, not your location.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan barcodes and to take meal photos for AI estimates. You can also choose a photo from your library. Barcode scanning happens on your phone. Meal photos are sent for analysis as described below. We do not keep your photos.

AI food estimates

For each AI request we keep a record of when it was made, the estimate that came back, and what it cost, so we can enforce daily limits and control spending. These records belong to your account and are deleted with it.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details: what the app was doing when it failed, the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version. Crash reports are set up not to include your name, email address, account ID, or anything you have logged.

Stored only on your phone

Your sign-in session is kept in the iPhone Keychain. The app also keeps a copy of recent data on the phone so it opens quickly and can show your home and lock screen widgets. Reminders such as the rest timer and the evening food reminder are scheduled on the phone itself; we do not send push notifications.

We use Google's paid Gemini service. Under its terms, Google does not use what we send to train its models, and keeps it only for a limited time to detect abuse and meet legal obligations.

A few accounts created before the public release can also connect WHOOP, to import workouts and heart-rate summaries, or send meal photos to a computer of their own instead of to Google. These options are not offered to new accounts.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people using hyPer. If hyPer were ever transferred to someone else, this policy would continue to apply to the data collected under it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account, so your history is there when you come back. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our database right away, including your AI request records. Copies in our database backups are removed as those backups expire, within 7 days.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to You, tap your name at the top, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it. If you signed in with Apple, it also tells Apple to stop the connection between your Apple Account and hyPer. If you cannot open the app, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything you have logged is visible and editable in the app.
  • Get a copy: write to us and we will send you a copy of your data.
  • Delete: delete individual entries in the app, or your whole account as described above.
  • Turn things off: you can turn off Apple Health, location, camera and photo access at any time in iOS Settings, and stop using AI estimates in the app. Logging by hand always works.

Depending on the state you live in, you may have further rights under its privacy law. We honor these requests for everyone, wherever they live, and will not treat you differently for making one. We will answer within 30 days.

Security

Data travels between the app and our servers encrypted. Our database is set up so that each account can only read and change its own data, and the keys to our AI and food-data services are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects it, as the law requires.

Age

hyPer is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Where hyPer is offered

hyPer is offered in the United States only. If you use it, your data is handled under United States law.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your existing data is used, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
