Train and eat to build muscle.

hyPer is an iPhone app that keeps your workouts, your food and your weekly training volume in one place.

Train

Run a program day or a free session set by set, with a rest timer, supersets and swaps. Your last session's numbers are there when you need them, and every muscle's weekly volume is counted against research-based ranges.

Fuel

Log food by search, barcode, saved meals or by hand, or describe or photograph a meal for an AI estimate you can check before saving. Calorie and protein targets come from a calculator and can follow your weight trend.

Progress

See every workout, edit past sessions and meals, and follow your weight with Apple Health. Home and lock screen widgets keep today in view.

hyPer is free, for adults in the United States. It is not medical advice.
